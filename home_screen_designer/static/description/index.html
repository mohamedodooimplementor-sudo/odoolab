THE HOME SCREEN

A home screen worthy of the name

A large clock with the date, a personal "Good morning" greeting, a search bar that finds the apps and their menus as you type, and the app grid on a background of your choice. On Odoo Community, the module adds this full screen home screen, opened after login and from the apps button. On Odoo Enterprise, it dresses up the native Enterprise home menu.

THE LIVE DESIGNER

Move a slider, see the result. Instantly.

One click on the designer icon of the top bar opens a side panel. Every change is applied behind it as you make it, before you even save. Save keeps it, Cancel puts everything back as it was.

Layout

3 to 12 columns, or fit the grid to the screen width. Spacing between the apps from 0 to 48 px.

Icons

Size from 32 to 128 px, corner radius from square to round, text size, names shown or hidden, text color automatic, light or dark.

Background

Odoo's default, one of 8 gradients or your own picture, with visibility, light or dark overlay and blur.

Effects

Glass tiles (glassmorphism) with adjustable blur, entrance animation, hover effect: lift, bounce or grow.

Greeting & clock

Personal greeting with the first name, clock with or without seconds, date. Each one can be turned off.

Behavior

Search bar on the apps and menus, home screen opened after login: on or off, as you like.

BACKGROUNDS

Eight moods. Or your own picture.

Eight hand-tuned gradients, light or dark. Prefer your office, your products or your brand? Upload any picture: it is resized by the browser before being sent, and the overlay keeps the texts readable on any image.

APPS ORDER

Your apps, in your order

Drag and drop the apps to put the ones you use every day first. One click on the eye hides an app you never open, from the home screen and from the apps bar. Hiding is only a matter of display: access rights do not change, and the app stays reachable from its links. One more click brings back Odoo's order.

INSIDE THE APPS

From CRM to Invoicing in one click

An apps bar on the left of every app: icons only, or icons and names. The current app is highlighted, the order and hidden apps follow your home screen. The top bar can keep your brand color, or blend with the page for a clean, modern look, in light and in dark mode. The bar steps aside on phones.

DARK MODE

The dark mode, now on Community too

Odoo Community has no dark theme: Home Screen Designer brings a complete dark palette, built on Odoo's own styles, for every view and every app. Each user picks Light, Dark or System (follows the computer or phone setting), from the user menu or from the panel. On Odoo Enterprise, the native dark mode is used and simply respected.

YOUR BRAND

Your colors everywhere. Your icon on every phone.

In the settings, pick your brand color, the primary color of the buttons and links, the top bar color, and the success, info, warning and danger colors. They are compiled into Odoo's own styles, not painted over them: every button, badge, link and progress bar follows, in every app. Brand, primary and top bar colors can get their own shade in dark mode. One click resets Odoo's palette.

Installable app: your icon, not Odoo's

Odoo can be installed as an app on Android, iPhone, Windows, macOS and ChromeOS. Upload your logo: it becomes the icon of the installed app, in every size the devices ask for (iPhone home screen included). Choose the color of the window title bar and of the splash screen too.

AUTOMATIC REFRESH

Data that stays fresh, by itself

A refresh button in every list, kanban, calendar, graph, pivot or form view reloads the data without reloading the page. Its menu turns on an automatic refresh: every 15 or 30 seconds, every 1, 2, 5, 10 or 30 minutes. Perfect for a helpdesk queue, a shop floor or a sales pipeline on the office TV. Each view remembers its own interval.

Smart: it never gets in your way

The automatic refresh pauses while the browser tab is hidden, while a dialog is open, while a list row is being edited or records are selected, and while a form has unsaved changes. No lost input, no lost selection, no useless load on the server.

Questions? Answers.

+  Does it work with Odoo Community and Enterprise?

Yes, both. On Community, the module adds its own full screen home screen. On Enterprise, it customizes the native home menu and uses Odoo's own dark mode. Odoo.sh and on-premise are supported. A version for Odoo 20 is also available.

+  Does it work with other themes?

Yes. When a backend theme already brings its own home menu or dark mode, the module detects it and steps aside instead of fighting it. The apps bar starts where the theme's content starts.

+  Can each user have a different home screen?

Yes. Every user can save a personal layout. The others keep the company layout, defined by the administrators, until they make their own.

+  Does hiding an app remove access to it?

No. Hiding only declutters the home screen and the apps bar of that user. Access rights are managed by Odoo's groups, as usual.

+  Does the automatic refresh load the server?

Barely. It is off by default, set per view, and only reloads the data of the view on screen. It pauses when the tab is hidden, so forgotten tabs cost nothing.

+  Who can change the theme colors and the app icon?

The administrators, in Settings → Home Screen. They apply to the whole database. The page reloads after saving so that everybody gets the new colors.

+  Does it work on phones?

Yes. The home screen adapts to small screens, and the apps bar steps aside to leave all the space to your data.

+  Which languages are included?

English and French, fully translatable with Odoo's standard tools.

Des questions ? Des réponses.

+  Fonctionne-t-il avec Odoo Community et Enterprise ?

Oui, les deux. Sur Community, le module ajoute son propre écran d'accueil plein écran. Sur Enterprise, il personnalise le menu d'accueil natif et utilise le mode sombre d'Odoo. Odoo.sh et les installations sur site sont pris en charge. Une version pour Odoo 20 est aussi disponible.

+  Fonctionne-t-il avec d'autres thèmes ?

Oui. Quand un thème apporte déjà son propre menu d'accueil ou mode sombre, le module le détecte et s'efface au lieu de le contrarier. La barre d'applications commence là où commence le contenu du thème.

+  Chaque utilisateur peut-il avoir un écran d'accueil différent ?

Oui. Chaque utilisateur peut enregistrer sa disposition personnelle. Les autres gardent la disposition de la société, définie par les administrateurs, jusqu'à ce qu'ils fassent la leur.

+  Masquer une application en retire-t-il l'accès ?

Non. Masquer désencombre seulement l'écran d'accueil et la barre d'applications de cet utilisateur. Les droits d'accès restent gérés par les groupes d'Odoo, comme d'habitude.

+  L'actualisation automatique charge-t-elle le serveur ?

À peine. Elle est désactivée par défaut, se règle vue par vue, et ne recharge que les données de la vue affichée. Elle se met en pause quand l'onglet est caché : les onglets oubliés ne coûtent rien.

+  Qui peut changer les couleurs du thème et l'icône ?

Les administrateurs, dans Paramètres → Home Screen. Elles s'appliquent à toute la base. La page se recharge après l'enregistrement pour que chacun ait les nouvelles couleurs.

+  Fonctionne-t-il sur téléphone ?

Oui. L'écran d'accueil s'adapte aux petits écrans, et la barre d'applications s'efface pour laisser toute la place à vos données.

+  Quelles langues sont incluses ?

Anglais et français, entièrement traduisible avec les outils standard d'Odoo.
